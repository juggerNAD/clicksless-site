Common questions

I’m not very techy. Is that a problem?

Not at all. I handle the setup. You’ll get a short video showing what happens, and you won’t need to change how you work day to day.

Do I need to buy new software?

Usually not. I build on what you already use. If something small is needed, like a texting number, I’ll explain the cost before anything is set up.

What does it cost?

You get a fixed price for your setup before any work starts. An optional monthly care plan covers changes and checkups if you want it.

Will the texts sound like a robot?

No. Every message is written in your business’s voice, and you approve the wording before it goes live.

How fast can it be running?

Most single automations are live within a few days of your approval. Bigger bundles take about a week or two.
